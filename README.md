# Smart Online | E-Commerce Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, and product insights. The project transforms sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations and filtering.

## 📊 Project Overview

Smart Online is a Power BI data analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product trends.

The report is organized into three interactive dashboard pages, allowing users to explore business performance from different perspectives.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

### 1. Sales Overview

Analyzes overall sales performance, regional sales distribution, product categories, monthly sales trends, and order status.

![Sales Overview Dashboard](sales-overview.png)

### 2. Customer Behavior

Explores customer demographics, sales channels, payment methods, purchase patterns, and customer-related metrics.

![Customer Behavior Dashboard](customer-behavior.png)

### 3. Product & Business Insights

Examines product and brand sales performance, sales by season, discount patterns, and average unit prices to support business analysis.

![Product and Business Insights Dashboard](product-business-insights.png)

## ✨ Key Features

- Interactive filters for sales season, order date, region, category, and sales channel.
- Sales performance analysis across regions and product categories.
- Monthly sales trend visualization.
- Customer behavior and demographic analysis.
- Product and brand performance comparisons.
- Discount and pricing analysis.
- Order status and sales channel analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and interactive data visualization.
- **Data Analytics** — Exploration of sales, customer, and product performance.
- **Data Visualization** — Charts, KPI cards, and interactive filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key sales performance indicators.
- Identify regional and product-level sales trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and sales.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
